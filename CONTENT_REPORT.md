# v3.0 Content Build

This build keeps the working v2.9 Firebase/Host/TV foundation and expands only the
content-selection and visual-display layer.

## Production banks

- Next Gen pool: 137
- Old School pool: 167
- Family Showdown pool: 40 (10 x 1-point, 10 x 2-point, 10 x 3-point, 10 x 4-point)
- Bonus Vault: 30
- Omega: 12
- Rapid Fire logo pool: 24
- Logo Challenge: 24
- Who Am I?: 15
- What Is This?: 15
- Name That Artist: 20
- Movie / TV Challenge: 20

## Selection rules

- Next Gen: 30 unique random questions per game.
- Old School: 30 unique random questions per game.
- Family Showdown: 20 questions total; 5 randomly selected from each point tier.
- Regular specials: 5 randomly selected questions per mini-round.
- Rapid Fire: shuffled logo pool, one logo at a time.
